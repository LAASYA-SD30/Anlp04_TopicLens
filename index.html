CONCLUSION

From word counts to latent themes.

TopicLens demonstrates text preprocessing, document-term representation, probabilistic topic modelling, sampling-based inference, topic-word distributions, and document-level topic interpretation.

This demonstration uses a small, predefined corpus and five topics. Evaluate topic quality using a larger document collection before drawing broader conclusions.
